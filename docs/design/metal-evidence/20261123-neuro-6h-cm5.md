Every hour of the single steady client (the Pi browsing the same 12 sites) was scored deviant. That follows from the
reference engine's cold start in `neuro-core`:
- each hour-of-day slot is cold until it has `SLOT_MIN` = 3 observations (≈ 3 days);
- a new peer is admitted only after `PERSIST_K` = 6 hours seen;

so on a fresh device, early hours full of not-yet-admitted CDN peers score high. N0 keeps these as evidence only
(no alert, no verdict, no action), so nothing user-visible happened. For the research session: a device's first
hours produce one deviation record per hour. Consider a warm-up suppression or a novelty discount while the slot is
cold before any evidence is surfaced (API P2 / Mission Control).
