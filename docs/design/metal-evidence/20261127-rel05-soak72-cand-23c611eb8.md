## Defect found: `el0-lansvc` functional probe fails on every round
- **What:** `[SUPERV] functional probe FAILED: el0-lansvc answered wrongly without faulting — reset (journaled, kind
  probe)` appeared **3,900 times in 72 h**, i.e. on every probe round (once per minute). The supervisor reset the
  LAN-services server each time.
- **Impact during the soak:** no measured service loss. DNS and DHCP answered in every sample, and there were no
  reboots and no degradation. The DNS latency tail (p99 423 ms) may be related and is checked in the follow-up.
- **When it started:**

  | Build | Probe failures |
  |---|---|
  | `am35B-78cb7095` (the bench image for days before this week) | 32 in total |
  | `cand-e478bca62` | 164 in about 3.6 h |
  | `cand-23c611eb8` | 3,900 in 72 h |

  `el0-lansvc`, its ABI and the probe did not change between those builds. The change is in the server's input or
  environment, most likely the clean flash (a factory-fresh configuration).
- **Mechanism (code):** the probe replays 72 known calls and compares each answer's digest with the one recorded from
  the server's first answers of the boot. One that fails every round means some command's answer depends on
  kernel-side data that changes, so the recorded reference goes stale.
- **Next:** an image whose probe-failure line names the known call (command, input, recorded and actual digests),
  then a fix of the probe or the server. Branch `diag/lansvc-probe-detail`. It needs one OTA on the bench.
