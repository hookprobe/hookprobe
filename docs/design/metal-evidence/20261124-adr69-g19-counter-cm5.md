# ADR 0069 G19 msg1 replay protection on CM5 + the live S0 test PoP (2026-11-24)

Operator: "do adr 69 metal test" (2026-11-24). This is the evidence plan in ADR 0069 (E2/E3) on the real device and PoP.

## Setup
- **PoP (S0 test, aarch64):** superpop built from #1558 (sha256 `941d1625…`). Before installing, it passed its selftest on the PoP. It runs with `--ctr-state /var/lib/superpop/ctr.txt` (unit: `StateDirectory=superpop`). The previous binary and unit are kept as `superpop.pre-adr69` and `superpop.service.pre-adr69`. The PoP stays on this build, which is the ship order: PoP first.

- **Old-format control:** `g19spect-e15487eb`, the pre-#1558 image.

### The one scripted BAD, and the re-check
The script's replay check compared `handshakes_ok` and `hs_stale_ctr` before and after. The PoP logs its counters once a minute, and the replay ran between the PoP restart and the new process's first stats line. So the "before" values came from the previous process, and the comparison failed. The capture filter (`udp dst port 7654`) also caught the PoP's own msg2 going back to the device. Replaying those two datagrams produced a 1,144-byte non-msg1, which the PoP rejected: that is the `handshakes_refused: 1`, an artifact of the test.

Clean re-check, 08:54–08:56 UTC on the PoP. Only the device's msg1 (3 datagrams, 2,488 B, counter 1) was replayed, with the stats read across full one-minute intervals:

```
before: handshakes_ok: 2, handshakes_refused: 1 hs_stale_ctr: 15
replayed 3 datagrams of the recorded msg1 (counter 1)
after:  handshakes_ok: 2, handshakes_refused: 1 hs_stale_ctr: 16
mark: 941ec1e589b046a5… 3
```

The replay was refused as stale (+1). No session was created, nothing else was refused, and the mark stayed at 3.

## Operational note
The bench CM5's mark on the S0 PoP is 3. Any image without #1558 can no longer bring up the tunnel to this PoP. That is the intended no-downgrade rule. Recovery: delete that device's line from `/var/lib/superpop/ctr.txt` with the PoP stopped (README).
