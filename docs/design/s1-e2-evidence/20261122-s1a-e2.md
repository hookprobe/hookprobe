## Open finding (not a blocker)

**F-S1-1:** the PoP counted `unknown_sid` 39 (data records under a session id it does not hold) and 5 handshakes for
the two guests, against `records_ok` 4. The guest appears to re-handshake and keep sending on the previous session
id. Also, ASSIGN took 47.5 s. To investigate with E1 (QEMU local, simulated PoP): the client's session-id
switch-over on re-handshake, and the retry/backoff timing before ASSIGN. No security impact observed (the PoP drops
those records); it costs tunnel bring-up time.
